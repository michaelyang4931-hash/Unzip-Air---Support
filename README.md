## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. I installed the app, but the right-click menu ("Extract Here with Unzip Air") is not showing up.
macOS requires you to manually enable Finder Extensions for privacy reasons. To enable it:
1. Open your Mac's **System Settings**.
2. Go to **Privacy & Security** -> **Extensions** -> **Added Extensions**.
3. Find **Unzip Air** in the list and check the box next to it.
4. Relaunch Finder (or restart your Mac) if it still doesn't appear.

### 2. How do I restore my Pro subscription?
If you have reinstalled the app or switched to a new Mac, simply open Unzip Air, click on **Upgrade to Pro** in the bottom left corner of the sidebar, and click the **"Restore here"** link at the bottom of the page. You will not be charged again.

### 3. How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?
All subscriptions and trials are managed securely by Apple. To cancel:
1. Open the **App Store** app on your Mac.
2. Click your name at the bottom left, then click **Account Settings**.
3. Scroll down to the **Manage** section and click **Manage** next to Subscriptions.
4. Find Unzip Air and click **Edit** to cancel.

### 4. Is my data safe? Does the app upload my files?
Yes, your data is 100% safe. Unzip Air operates entirely offline. It does not upload, sync, or transmit your files to any cloud servers. All extraction and compression tasks are performed locally using your Mac's own processor.
