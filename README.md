Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. I installed the app, but the right-click menu ("Extract Here with Unzip Air") is not showing up.
macOS requires you to manually enable Finder Extensions for privacy reasons. To enable it:

Open your Mac's System Settings.

Go to Privacy & Security -> Extensions -> Added Extensions.

Find Unzip Air in the list and check the box next to it.

Relaunch Finder (or restart your Mac) if it still doesn't appear.

2. I purchased the "Lifetime Pro," but it's still locked. How do I restore it?
If you have reinstalled the app or switched to a new Mac, you can restore your one-time purchase:

Open Unzip Air.

Click on Upgrade to Pro in the sidebar.

Click the "Already purchased? Restore here" link at the bottom of the page.

Since this is a Lifetime Purchase, you will never be charged again once restored.

3. Is this a subscription or a one-time purchase?
Unzip Air Pro is a one-time, lifetime purchase. There are no recurring monthly or annual fees. Once you upgrade, you own all current and future Pro features permanently.

4. How do I manage my purchase history?
All transactions are handled securely by Apple. You can view your purchase history:

Open the App Store app on your Mac.

Click your name at the bottom left, then click Account Settings.

Look for Purchase History and click Manage or See All.
